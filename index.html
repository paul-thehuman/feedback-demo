The Feedback Dilemma

A 6-minute interactive on giving feedback that lands. Built as a portfolio demo. No fluff, just useful.

Scenario: Alex has missed two deadlines. You’ve got five minutes before your next meeting. What now?

What you'll practice

Three habits that make feedback stick:

Clarity over catharsis SBI framing Curiosity first

Quick choice: what's your move?

There’s no perfect answer here. Just trade-offs.

0
Delay it. Send a message: “Let’s talk next week.”

Signal: avoidance. Problem lingers. You feel better for 12 minutes.

1
Be blunt. “You’re dropping the ball.”

Signal: urgency but vague. Likely defensiveness, little learning.

2
Explore first. “Can we check in for five minutes?”

Signal: curiosity + respect. Keeps door open for facts.

Mini-concept: the SBI frame

S Situation — name the moment. B Behavior — say what you saw. I Impact — explain the effect.

You: “Yesterday in the client review (S), you joined 8 minutes late (B), which meant we missed your input on risks (I).”

Not this: “You’re unreliable.”

Say it. Pick your phrasing.

Alex is in front of you. Choose your opening line.

0
“You’re being unprofessional and it’s hurting the team.”
1
“Missing deadlines isn’t acceptable.”
2
“On the Q3 deck yesterday, the slide deadlines slipped twice, which pushed the client send. Can we talk about what got in the way?”

Follow up. What next?

Alex crosses arms: “I had too many priorities.” Your move:

0
“Why didn’t you plan better?”
1
“From now on, send me your plan every morning.”
2
“What collided with the deadlines? Let’s map priorities and reset realistically.”

Close it out

Time’s up soon. How will you land this?

0
“Please try harder next week.”
1
“Let’s aim to improve deadlines going forward.”
2
“We’ll lock a 30-min priority review on Mondays, and you’ll flag risk by Thursday noon. I’ll unblock where needed.”

Your result

0 / 6

Score 5–6: you balanced clarity and care. 3–4: decent, but tighten the close. 0–2: you’re venting, not leading.

Download: Feedback Cheat-Sheet

  • ✔ Prepare SBI: Situation, Behavior, Impact in one sentence each.
  • ✔ Lead with curiosity: two what/how questions before advice.
  • ✔ Agree next steps: one habit + one checkpoint + one date.
