Before you start

SBI keeps feedback about behaviour, not character

S

Situation. Name the specific moment. "In the last fortnight..."

B

Behaviour. What you observed, not what you concluded. "...the Henderson brief and Tuesday's report came in late..." Never "...you've been sloppy."

I

Impact. The concrete effect. "...so the client saw a draft that wasn't ready."

Then stop talking. SBI opens the conversation. Curiosity earns the truth.
